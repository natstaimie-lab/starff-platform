# Starff marketing

Working files for Starff's branded marketing material and launch.

**The plan lives in Claude Docs:** *Starff Marketing Plan & Launch* —
https://claude.ai/code/artifact/e98395dd-63ad-453c-9747-2fdfd7ccf5ca

That document is the source of truth for positioning, audiences, channels, the
launch timeline and compliance. This folder holds the written material that
supports it. Canva holds anything visual.

## Where things live

| What | Where |
|------|-------|
| Plan, positioning, timeline | Claude doc (link above) |
| Editable designs | Canva |

## Blocked on decisions

Three things must be settled before any asset ships. All three are in the
plan's "Brand system" section:

1. The real phone number — the flyer currently carries `+44 20 7946 0123`,
   which is in Ofcom's reserved fictional range and reaches nobody.
2. The sector list — the website says four sectors, the flyer says six.
3. Whether "Get Starffed" is the main line or a campaign line.

## Compliance — applies to every worker-facing advert

Build these into templates as fixed fields, not optional ones:

- Full registered business name and contact details
- The word "temporary"
- Rate, location, and what the role requires
- Never advertise a named client's vacancy without their written authorisation
- Never charge a work-seeker a fee

Sources and detail are in the plan's "Compliance guardrails" section.
